Scope & limitations
Scope & limitationsUnderstand the prototype boundaries and known limitations.

Architecture Boundaries & Known Limitations:

Prototype behaviour: This demo presents read-only interactions with synthetic data. It does not prove production data controls.
Catalog Boundaries: Indexed operational data spans calendar years 2023–2026. A fiscal-year calendar is not configured. Pre-2023 payroll and internal corporate HR records (stored in Workday) are out-of-scope.
Ungrounded Forecasting: External macroeconomic forecasts (e.g. 2030 mortgage interest rates) are unsupported to prevent hallucination.
Simulated access and masking: Persona-based restrictions and masked fields illustrate intended behaviour only; no production enforcement is implied.
